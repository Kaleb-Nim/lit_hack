Nobody was unaware

the gap is not alerting

Singapore publishes provision-level legislative change as a free machine-readable feed. The Employment Act feed alone carries 667 provision records. Every firm can already see what changed.

Signed April 2026 · still in force You hereby consent to the Employer collecting, using, processing and/or disclosing any or all personal data relevant to your internship…
Consent stopped being the right instrument on 1 February 2021. Five years of signatures later, the clause is unchanged.
The check that needs no model Roman-numeral PDPA Part references — “Parts III to VI” — and citations into ss 27–35.
The Act was renumbered to Arabic in the 2020 Revised Edition and those sections were repealed. The numerals alone date a document as pre-2021. Regex, not reasoning.

The gap was never awareness. Nothing connected the amendment to these files.

  • Discovered, not entered. Every incumbent requires this edge to pre-exist as human data entry. This one is inferred from documents that never declared it.
  • It persists, so it compounds. The hundredth amendment costs a fraction of the first, because the edges are already there. That is the difference between a tool and an asset.
  • Only on-premises makes it possible. No vendor is permitted to index a firm’s whole cross-client corpus. That is why this gap is still open.

Four states, and no fifth

in what way it is affected

A finding is an enum, a statutory quote and a document span. Prose is secondary. There is no compliant state — the ledger cannot represent a clause as safe.

Contradicted

The clause routes the statutory access channel to a department. Section 11(3) requires a designated individual, and s 11(5) requires publishing their contact information.

Insufficient

Cross-border transfer promises “reasonable steps”. Regulation 11 admits four routes, and reasonable steps is none of them. True, but no longer enough.

Stale

Built on consent when the employment exception has been available since 2021. Worse than cosmetic: a consent can be withdrawn under s 16, the exception cannot.

Unreviewed

No edge has been assessed against this provision. Reported as a count, never as reassurance. Silence is not evidence of compliance.

Same rule, different failure

why one patch is the wrong patch

Regulation 11 hits two documents. A tool that proposed the same edit to both would be wrong.

DOC-001 · eyewear group the Employer will take reasonable steps to ensure your personal data continues to receive a standard of protection comparable to that under the Act.
Weak on transfer. Strong nowhere else either — no enumerated purposes, no contact point. Remedy: rebuild on binding corporate rules naming destination countries.
DOC-002 · consultancy where the transfer of data outside Singapore is involved, appropriate measures are taken to ensure that adequate safeguards are in place.
Stronger on notification — purposes in-line, an annex of data types and recipients. Remedy: leave the notification limb alone, replace only the transfer limb.
An organisation must, before transferring personal data outside Singapore, take appropriate steps to ensure that the recipient is bound by legally enforceable obligations to provide a standard of protection comparable to that under the Act.PDP Regulations 2021, reg 11 · sso.agc.gov.sg

One instrument. Two answers.

the null result

Act 19 of 2025 Statutes (Miscellaneous Amendments) Act — one commencement date, 5 December 2025, amending both Acts this corpus depends on.

PDPA · section 22 of the amending Act 0 affected

Deletes one sub-paragraph on disclosure of a patient’s data by a licensed healthcare institution. A tidy-up after the Healthcare Services Act transition. No clause here relies on it.

Employment Act · section 7 of the same Act 1 insufficient

Inserts two further lawful grounds for salary deduction, under the MediShield Life and CareShield Life schemes. The deductions clause under-states the grounds now available.

An alerting tool fires twice here. This fires once, and explains the silence. The difference is that we reason about provisions, not Acts.

The practice nobody wrote down

accumulated working practices

The brief’s hardest clause. Habits live in no system — but they leave fingerprints across the corpus.

One edge, four documents Both confidentiality clauses impose a comprehensive duty of silence and create no internal reporting route.
Section 26D(1) gives three calendar days from assessment to notify the Commission. Assessment happens because a member of staff escalates. An intern who notices a breach is contractually instructed to keep quiet. Compliance with a statutory deadline depends on a path the contract never builds.
  • The ledger found it by the edge, not the wording — four documents, four different drafters, one shared assumption.
  • It is in no playbook, no checklist and no training material. It is a habit, and this is what a habit looks like when you can finally see it.

Propagate, carefully

before the outdated version causes harm

A signed contract is not a text file. Varying it is a legal act requiring the counterparty. The artefact type decides what propagation even means.

ExecutedRisk register

Cannot be edited unilaterally. It becomes a logged exposure with a remediation owner, surfaced at renewal.

TemplateRedline

The highest-leverage fix. Patching the precedent fixes every unwritten future document; patching a signed contract fixes one.

DraftBlock

Caught before execution. This is the only point where the harm is genuinely prevented rather than recorded.

  • A lawyer accepts or declines every edit. The diff is the unit of review, and the acceptance is the audit record.
  • Coverage is reported, not implied. Mapped obligations and unmapped ones are both counted, on screen.

What we will not claim

constraints, stated as confidence
  • Singapore Statutes Online is not authoritative. The Gazette is. We pin to SSO because it is versioned and free, and we say so.
  • Guidance cannot be version-pinned. The PDPC does not version its advisory guidelines — the NRIC guidelines still read “last updated 31 August 2018” while the policy moved through advisories. A statutory assertion can be proved stale. A guidance assertion can only be flagged unverifiable. We record which is which.
  • The assertions are hand-authored, by design. A model never writes an obligation. It proposes edges and drafts language; the rule itself is human, versioned and reviewable.
  • The model cannot say “compliant”. Not because we filtered it out, but because the finding type has no such value. The catastrophic failure is structurally unrepresentable.
